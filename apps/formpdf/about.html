About FormPdf

Smaller images. Easier document uploads.

FormPdf is a simple Android utility for compressing images and preparing files for online forms, applications, and everyday sharing.

Choose an image, set your preferred size in KB or MB, and compress it directly on your device.

Features

  • Custom size: Set a target size for individual images or multiple images.
  • Document presets: Start with convenient targets for Aadhaar, PAN, photos, and signatures.
  • Camera capture: Take photos, review them, and optionally crop before compression.
  • Multiple images: Add, remove, and reorder images before processing.
  • Clear results: Preview compressed files and compare their original and resulting sizes.
  • PDF export: Save one or multiple compressed images together as a PDF.
  • File management: View saved results, rename files, mark favorites, share, and delete.
  • Appearance options: Choose Light, Dark, or System theme.

Processing on Your Device

Image compression and PDF creation happen on your device. No account or document upload to our servers is required. Core processing works offline; advertisements and external services require an internet connection.

FormPdf displays banner advertisements on the Home screen to support development.

Please Note

Final file size depends on the source image and compression settings. Presets are convenient starting points and do not guarantee acceptance by any website or organization.

FormPdf is an independent utility and is not affiliated with UIDAI, government departments, or PAN-issuing authorities.
