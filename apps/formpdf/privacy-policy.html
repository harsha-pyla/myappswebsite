This Privacy Policy explains how FormPdf handles information when you use its Android application.

1. Images and Documents

FormPdf accesses images, documents, and camera captures that you select or create for processing.

Compression, previews, and PDF creation take place on your device. FormPdf does not upload your image or document contents to servers operated by us or provide those contents to advertising services.

The app may read file information such as filenames, file sizes, dimensions, and orientation to display and process your files.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

FormPdf stores information locally to provide its features, including:

  • Imported working files, compressed outputs, previews, and temporary files.
  • Saved file history and related information.
  • Favorite status and renamed filenames.
  • Preferences such as appearance and the last selected target size.

You do not need to create an account to use FormPdf. We do not operate a cloud account or document-storage service for the app.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: Requested when you use camera capture. You can decline or revoke access in Android settings.
  • Selected files: Android's photo picker and document picker let you choose which files to provide. FormPdf does not request unrestricted access to your entire photo library or storage.
  • Internet and network access: Used by advertising and consent services. Core image compression and PDF creation are performed locally.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

FormPdf uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements on its Home screen.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information including IP addresses, advertising and other device identifiers, app or ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. An IP address may be used to estimate a device's general location. Data handling can depend on consent, device settings, and SDK configuration. See developers.google.com for details.

FormPdf does not include a separate developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service. However, advertising SDKs have their own measurement and diagnostic practices.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps using its services

5. Advertising Choices

FormPdf uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent where applicable.

When available, you can revisit your choices through Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also manage advertising identifiers and relevant privacy controls in your Android settings.

Changing advertising preferences does not delete files stored locally by FormPdf.

6. Saving, Sharing, and External Services

Files are shared only when you choose a sharing action. The receiving app or service handles those files under its own privacy practices.

When saving through Android's document picker, you may choose a device folder or a cloud-storage provider. A cloud provider may upload your file according to its own settings and policies.

External links, advertisements, and app-store links may open services that we do not control.

7. Retention and Deletion

Saved history remains on your device until you remove it, clear the app's history, clear app storage, or uninstall the app.

Temporary processing and sharing copies may remain in app storage or cache until cleaned up. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes the app's private local data.

Deleting history does not delete copies you previously exported, saved elsewhere, or shared with another service.

Information handled by Google or another external service is subject to that service's retention and deletion practices.

8. Security

FormPdf uses Android's app-storage protections and grants receiving apps access to shared files through Android's sharing mechanism.

No device, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device and take care when sharing sensitive documents.

9. Support Communications and Privacy Requests

If you contact us, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to your request.

Avoid sending identity documents or other sensitive files unless necessary for your support request.

Contact harshavardhan1029p@gmail.com to ask questions or request deletion of support correspondence. We cannot remotely access or delete files stored only on your device.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
